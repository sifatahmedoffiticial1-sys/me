About Me

Turning repetitive work into intelligent, self-running systems.

I'm Sifat Ahmed, an AI Automation Engineer and Workflow Specialist who designs, builds, and ships automation systems for businesses that want to move faster without adding headcount. My work sits at the intersection of AI agents, API integrations, and no-code/low-code automation platforms.

My Mission is to remove repetitive, manual work from businesses so teams can focus on decisions that actually need a human. My Vision is a future where every business, regardless of size, has access to enterprise-grade automation infrastructure.

Over the years I've worked across CRM automation, WhatsApp and email automation, RAG-based knowledge agents, and internal business tools — always with the same standard: reliable, scalable, and built to be maintained, not just demoed.

My Work Philosophy is simple — discover deeply, architect clearly, build in small verifiable steps, and optimize after launch, not before. Businesses trust me because I communicate clearly, ship on time, and build systems that keep working long after the project ends.
